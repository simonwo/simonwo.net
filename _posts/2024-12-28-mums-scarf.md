Made on an Ashford rigid heddle loom. From Jamieson’s Shetland Marl Chunky yarn (in Highland Mist, Teviot and Bering Sea)

Needless to say I am extremely pleased with this! The colours are so rich. It was a really fast project, maybe only 7-8 sessions of weaving. The recipient (mum) is very happy! Also got the tension right on this one so its light but still warm. I still suck at getting the fringe to be the same length. I don’t know why that is so hard. I am now ordering more wool to do one for myself lol.
